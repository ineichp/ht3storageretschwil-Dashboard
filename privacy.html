Privacy Policy

Storage Retschwil

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Storage Retschwil is a private monitoring and control application intended for personal use by the owner of the Storage Retschwil installation. The app provides access to environmental measurements, surveillance events, device status, energy information, cost information, and selected device controls.

Information processed by the app

The app may process the following information in order to provide the dashboard functionality:

  • Account login information, such as the authorized user email address.
  • Authentication session data required to keep the dashboard signed in.
  • Temperature, humidity, flood sensor, battery, cable, power, and device status readings.
  • Camera upload metadata, video object references, and Amazon Rekognition detection labels and confidence values.
  • Energy usage and cost information shown in the dashboard.
  • AWS service cost information shown in the dashboard.
  • Push notification tokens if app notifications are enabled on the Android device.

Biometric authentication

Biometric authentication, such as fingerprint unlock, is handled locally by Android. The app does not receive, store, transmit, or otherwise access biometric templates or fingerprint data.

How the information is used

The information is used only to operate the private Storage Retschwil dashboard, including:

  • Displaying live and historical measurements.
  • Showing device state, online status, flood status, and energy usage.
  • Displaying video detection events and relevant labels.
  • Sending configured alerts through push notifications or WhatsApp.
  • Controlling authorized Shelly devices from the dashboard.

Third-party services

The app and backend use third-party services to provide the private dashboard:

  • Amazon Web Services for hosting, authentication, APIs, storage, logging, monitoring, and video recognition.
  • Google Play services for app distribution and, if enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging push notifications.
  • Shelly Cloud for connected device state and control.
  • CallMeBot/WhatsApp for configured WhatsApp alert delivery.

Data sharing

The app is not designed to sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared only with the infrastructure and service providers required to operate the private dashboard and notifications.

Data retention

Operational logs, measurements, videos, detection events, and cost data are retained according to the configured Storage Retschwil AWS retention and lifecycle settings. Some video and detection data may be automatically deleted after the configured retention period or when it is no longer relevant for the dashboard.

Security

Access to the dashboard is restricted to authorized users. The backend uses AWS authentication and access controls. No security measure is perfect, but the app is designed for restricted private use rather than public social or advertising use.

Children

The app is not intended for children and is not directed at children. It is intended only for the private operator of the Storage Retschwil installation.
